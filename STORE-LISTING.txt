FULL DESCRIPTION  (4000 char limit)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ARVANN is a B2B marketplace built for Indian manufacturing. Source raw materials, find verified suppliers, negotiate quotes, and run your books - from one app.

BROWSE A REAL MANUFACTURING CATALOGUE
Search products across 16+ industries including textiles, paper and packaging, chemicals, metals, plastics, auto components, pharma, and food processing. Filter by category, price, and specification to find exactly what your line needs.

SOURCE WITH CONFIDENCE
Every listing shows the supplying company, its verification status, and full product specifications. Verified sellers display a trust badge earned by submitting GST and identity documentation for review.

REQUEST QUOTES, NOT GUESSWORK
Send a structured RFQ with your quantity, target price, and deadline. Sellers respond with unit pricing, minimum order quantity, lead time, and validity - so you can compare offers properly instead of chasing phone calls.

TALK DIRECTLY TO SELLERS
Message any supplier in real time. Product enquiries open with the item pinned to the conversation, so both sides know exactly what is being discussed. Share images and documents inline.

GST-READY BOOKKEEPING
Raise sales invoices and purchase bills with automatic CGST, SGST, and IGST calculation based on party state. Record receipts and payments, and track what you are owed with aged outstanding reports.

Run the reports your accountant asks for: Profit and Loss, GST Summary, Trial Balance, Party Outstanding, Stock Summary, and Stock Ledger.

INVENTORY THAT MATCHES YOUR BOOKS
Track stock across your catalogue with product variants, low-stock alerts, and a full movement history. Every invoice updates inventory automatically, so your stock position and your ledger never drift apart.

LIST YOUR OWN PRODUCTS
Publish your catalogue with photos, specifications, and variant pricing. Buyers find you by category and reach you by chat or call.

BUSINESS SERVICES
Request machine repair, skilled workforce, or transport and fleet coordination directly through the app, and track each request to completion.

BUILT FOR INDIAN BUSINESS
Pricing in INR. GST-compliant invoicing. Multi-company support for operators running more than one entity.

HOW BUYING WORKS
ARVANN connects buyers and sellers - it does not process payments. You enquire, negotiate, and agree commercial terms directly with the other business, then settle between yourselves. No commission, no payment gateway in between.
